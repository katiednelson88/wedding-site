Step three

Getting around

Rent a car. Even better, rent one as a group. Taxis and Ubers are very limited out here, and a car lets you go on adventures Friday before the wedding and Saturday before the reception.

  • Shuttles run between the lodges and the venue on Friday and Saturday
  • Scotland drives on the left. Consider an automatic if you're not used to a stick shift.

Questions

FAQ

Can I bring my kids?

Yes! Let us know on your RSVP that they're coming and whether they'll need their own seat.

Do I have to stay at the lodges?

No, but it's the easiest option, and transport is included Friday and Saturday. Let us know your plans by 1 March 2027.

What if I can only come for one day?

Totally fine. Pick the days you'll be there on the RSVP.

Is the Sunday debrief for everyone?

Yes. It's optional and very low key. Wear literally anything.

Do you have a registry?

Your presence is the present. Seriously, you're flying to Scotland.
