Where to stay

Staying at the lodges is highly recommended: we'll run transport back and forth to the venue on Friday and Saturday.

Tell us your plans

Alternate options

There is also a set of 4 chalets which have 3 bedrooms each. These are along the route to between the lodges and Daniel's house and will be included in the transportation route. If you have a group of 18 you think this would suit, let us know!!

There are a few Airbnbs available on the road between Daniel's house and the lodges that transportation can stop at. Keep in mind that Scotland has a 0 tolerance policy on drinking and driving, so if you're staying somewhere else plan to have a sober driver.

Step three

Getting around

We recommend renting a car when you arrive at Inverness airport. Even better, rent one as a group. Taxis and Ubers are very limited out here, and a car lets you go on adventures Friday before the wedding and Saturday before the reception.

  • Shuttles will run between the lodges and the venue on Friday and Saturday
  • Scotland drives on the left. PLS BE CAREFUL
  • Check back in as the date gets closer for info on where to rent.

Questions

FAQ

Can I bring my kids?

Yes! Let us know on your RSVP that they're coming and whether they'll need their own seat.

Do I have to stay at the lodges?

No, but it's the easiest option, and transport is included Friday and Saturday. Let us know your plans by 1 March 2027.

What if I can only come for one day?

Totally fine. Pick the days you'll be there on the RSVP.

Is the Sunday debrief for everyone?

Yes. It's optional and very low key. Wear literally anything.

Do you have a registry?

No gifts, you're literally flying to Scotland. That said, if you write a card, Katie is almost guaranteed to cry.
